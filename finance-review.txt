FINANCE MODULE REVIEW
=====================
Scope: FinancePaymentController, FinanceService, CommissionService,
DistributorPayoutService, FinanceInsightService, FinanceReportService,
PaymentPolicy, payout controllers.
Method: code reading only. Nothing was run and no finance code was changed.


BROKEN
------

1. Reversing a payment always returns 403.
   reverse() calls authorize('update', $payment). PaymentPolicy::update only
   allows 'pending' payments, but only 'confirmed' payments can be reversed.
   The "Reverse payment" button on the order page is a dead end.

2. Confirming a payment needs the wrong permission.
   The route requires confirm_payments, but the policy check inside also needs
   record_payments. A user with only confirm_payments gets a 403.
   Confirm and reverse need their own policy methods.

3. A payment can be reversed more than once.
   The original stays 'confirmed' and nothing checks for an existing reversal.
   Each click adds another negative payment and ledger entry.

4. Reversed payments still count as money received.
   - A reversal is a separate row with status 'reversed'; the original stays
     'confirmed'.
   - Every total sums only 'confirmed' rows, so the reversal is never netted out.
   - Affected: the order paid check in confirmPayment, invoice balances
     (syncInvoiceRegister), the aging report, client lifetime spend, and the
     dashboard "confirmed receipts".
   - A reversed order stays is_paid, with its commission and payable intact.
   - Refunds against an already-reversed payment are still allowed.

5. Commission payouts always fail.
   - CommissionService::calculate() creates entries with recipient_id = null and
     nothing ever assigns a recipient.
   - settle() requires entry.recipient_id to equal the chosen recipient, so
     every allocation is rejected.
   - Distributor side: distributor_share is hard-coded to 0, so those payables
     are always zero.

6. Refunds cannot be created.
   No code creates a Refund, so confirmRefund has nothing to confirm.

7. "Commission earned: on delivery" setting does nothing.
   Nothing reads commission_earned_trigger, and entries never move from
   'pending' to 'earned'.


CONTROL AND INTEGRITY
---------------------

8. No separation of duties.
   - The person who recorded a payment can confirm it.
   - One person with manage_finance records and approves a payout in one step
     (approved_by = recorded_by).
   - confirmRefund overwrites recorded_by with the confirmer, losing the
     original recorder.

9. Marking an order paid bypasses finance.
   orders/{order}/mark-paid needs only manage_orders. It sets is_paid with no
   payment record, ledger entry, commission or payable.

10. Payment inputs are not validated.
    - invoice_id is not checked to belong to the order.
    - The order is not checked to be open (cancelled orders accept payments).
    - Overpayment is not rejected.
    - reporting_amount is user-typed, not calculated from amount x rate.
    - The paid check adds amounts in whatever currency was entered and compares
      them to grand_total, so a payment in another currency can mark the order
      paid.

11. Payout currency is not checked.
    The batch currency is not compared with the currency of the entries or
    payables being paid. Batch totals are summed as floats.

12. Smaller issues.
    - Money is handled as float in paid checks and payout math.
    - reporting_currency is not uppercased.
    - updateSettings repeats the permission check the route already does.
    - markAsPaid throws a bare Exception, so a double-pay race gives a 500.


SUGGESTED FIX ORDER
-------------------

1. Fix the policy: separate confirm and reverse methods, no 'pending'
   requirement on reverse.                                         (items 1, 2)
2. Block double reversals and net them out everywhere (mark the original
   'reversed' or sum signed amounts), then revert is_paid.        (items 3, 4)
3. Add recipient and distributor-share assignment so payouts work. (item 5)
4. Add separation of duties and input validation.          (items 8, 10, 11)
5. Route mark-paid through the finance flow, or require confirm_payments.
                                                                    (item 9)

Needs a design decision first: items 5, 6 and 7 (recipient model, refund-entry
flow, "earned on delivery" behaviour) do not exist yet.
